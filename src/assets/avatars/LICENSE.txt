"Gaze" by DiceBear (https://www.dicebear.com), licensed CC0 1.0.
Colors are taken from the Hot Streak palette. Rows of five share a background:
Flip Three yellow, Second Chance red, felt green, Freeze blue, plum.
Each body has a 4px ink (#262451) outline and a 4px ink offset shadow, like the
app's cards and buttons. Triangle and diamond shapes and small and tall eyes
are left out because they don't read at seat size.
