# Sound credits

The sound effects in this folder are trimmed, loudness-matched and combined from these recordings. `table-loop` is the game's own music.

## Kenney

[Casino Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio) and [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under [Creative Commons Zero (CC0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

- `reveal`: card-place
- `deal`: card-fan
- `bank`: chips-stack
- `special`: card-shove
- `bust`: card-slide, with Button Sounds' low-thumps
- `round`: card-slide
- `flipSeven`: confirmation_004 and chips-handle
- `win`: confirmation_002, confirmation_004 and chips-handle
- `turn`: confirmation_001

## Button Sounds

[Button Sounds](https://github.com/joshwcomeau/button-sounds) by Josh Comeau.

- `freeze`: lock-closed
- `bust`: low-thumps
- `join`: guitar-toggle-on
- `message`: popper-empty
- `error`: tonal-off
- `tap`: hi75-soft
